Our Origin

YEARS IN THE MAKING.
FINALLY HERE.

The S.A.F.E. Programs Community Center is the culmination of Coach Elwood's lifelong mission — a place where children, adults, and seniors can come together to grow stronger in body, mind, and character.

After years of financial hardship and unwavering dedication, this non-profit organization has achieved what many thought impossible: a fully operational community center in the heart of New Hampshire, built entirely on passion for the community.

We believe that when kids are safe, active, and learning, families thrive. Our center gives parents peace of mind — bring your kids while you work or work out, knowing they're in good hands with certified coaches who genuinely care.

OUR PROGRAMS & FACILITIES

World-class facilities for every age. Children and adults alike will find their sport, their fitness, and their community under one roof.

🏓

Indoor Pickleball

Our climate-controlled indoor pickleball courts are open to all skill levels. Join leagues, take lessons, or just rally with friends.

⚾

Baseball

Training facilities for youth and adult players. Develop your swing, fielding, and pitching with experienced coaching staff.

🏀

Basketball

Full-court basketball for pickup games, leagues, and youth development programs. Fast breaks and fundamentals for all ages.

⛳

Golf Range Practice

Sharpen your short game and perfect your swing at our indoor golf practice range. Open to beginners and seasoned golfers alike.

🤼

Wrestling

Youth and adult wrestling programs focused on discipline, strength, and competitive skill development in a safe environment.

The S.A.F.E. Programs Community Center is designed for busy families. While you're at work or getting your own workout in at Rockin' Fitness, your kids are active, supervised, and building skills.

  • ✓
    Safe, supervised environment — Certified coaches and staff dedicated to every child's growth and wellbeing.
  • ✓
    Healthy habits from day one — Physical activity, nutrition awareness, and social skills taught through sport.
  • ✓
    Anti-bullying education — Our signature S.A.F.E. curriculum builds confidence and resilience against bullying.
  • ✓
    For all ages — Programs designed for children, adults, and seniors alike. The whole family belongs here.
  • ✓
    Charity memberships available — As a 501(c)(3), donations to our organization can come with membership recognition and benefits.
